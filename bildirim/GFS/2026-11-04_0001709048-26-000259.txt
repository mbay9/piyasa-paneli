GlobalFoundries Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Malta, New York , November 4, 2026 - GLOBALFOUNDRIES Inc. (GF) (Nasdaq: GFS) today announced preliminary
financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Key Third Quarter Financial Highlights
• Revenue of $1.899 billion
• Gross margin of 30.1% and Non-IFRS gross margin (1) of 31.4%
• Operating margin of 12.1% and Non-IFRS operating margin (1) of 17.9%
• Net income (loss) of $(270) million and Non-IFRS net income (1) of $298 million
• Diluted earnings (loss) per share ("EPS") of $(0.49) and Non-IFRS EPS (1) of $0.53
• Non-IFRS adjusted EBITDA (1) of $647 million
• Ending cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities of $3.2 billion
• Net cash provided by operating activities of $341 million and Non-IFRS adjusted free cash flow (1) of $136 million
"We delivered strong results in the third quarter, with record non-IFRS gross margin reflecting a richer mix of business across high-value, high-growth AI applications. We saw particularly strong momentum across our key growth markets, led by record year-over-year revenue growth in Communications Infrastructure and Data Center, as well as strong growth in Automotive and Home and Industrial IoT,” said Tim Breen, CEO of GlobalFoundries. "Together with the significant customer and government partnerships announced during the quarter, these results demonstrate the continued strategic diversification of GF’s business and the strength of our differentiated technologies."
Recent Business Highlights
• In October 2026, GF was selected as the technology provider and operator for two new semiconductor fabs at its Malta, New York site under a landmark U.S.-Japan initiative that will provide up to $21 billion in funding. GF will contribute its differentiated technologies, manufacturing expertise and operational capabilities, for which it will be compensated through management and technology fees over the life of the project. An initial investment of approximately $10 billion will accelerate construction of a new 300mm semiconductor fab, which is already underway, with capacity expected to come online in 2029. Together, the two fabs will add significant new U.S. manufacturing capacity and reinforce GF's ability to support customers worldwide.
• In October 2026, GF and the U.S. government ("USG") agreed to amend GF’s existing CHIPS and Science Act award, reallocating $955 million of incentives to support GF’s investments in advanced semiconductor manufacturing at its facilities in Malta, New York and Burlington, Vermont. The funding will help accelerate investments in silicon photonics, silicon germanium, and advanced packaging, including the first U.S.-based source of silicon interposers.
• In October 2026, GF entered into a multi-year, $2 billion manufacturing agreement with TSMC to establish a U.S.-based supply of silicon interposers for TSMC’s CoWoS advanced packaging ecosystem. Under the agreement, GF will add manufacturing capacity at its Malta, New York facility, with volume production expected to begin ramping in the first half of 2028. The collaboration expands GF’s advanced packaging capabilities and establishes a U.S.-based source of silicon interposers to support growing demand for AI and high-performance computing systems.
• In September 2026, GF issued 9.9 million ordinary shares to the USG, resulting in a $441 million non-cash charge recorded in non-operating expense, which was the primary driver of the IFRS net loss in the quarter.
• In September 2026, GF expanded its multi-year agreement with Marvell to increase manufacturing capacity for GF’s high-performance silicon germanium (SiGe) technology at its Burlington, Vermont facility. The

expanded capacity will support Marvell’s growing requirements for next-generation pluggable optical transceivers, Near-Packaged Optics (NPO) and Co-Packaged Optics (CPO), strengthening GF’s position in high-speed optical connectivity for AI and cloud data center infrastructure.
• In September 2026, GF entered into a long-term manufacturing agreement with Monolithic Power Systems (MPS) to deploy MPS’s proprietary process technology at GF’s 300mm manufacturing facility in Singapore, with volume production planned for early 2027. The partnership expands manufacturing capacity for high-performance power management solutions, including next-generation products for automotive architectures, industrial robotics and automation, and smart power stages for AI and cloud infrastructure.
Dividend Payment
On October 27, 2026, the Board of Directors approved a dividend of $0.12 per share payable on January 14, 2027 to shareholders of record as of December 22, 2026.

Financial Measures (Non-IFRS)
In addition to the financial information presented in accordance with International Financial Reporting Standards ("IFRS"), this press release includes the following Non-IFRS financial measures: Non-IFRS gross profit, Non-IFRS operating profit, Non-IFRS operating expense, Non-IFRS net income, Non-IFRS selling, general and administrative, Non-IFRS research and development, Non-IFRS other income (expense), Non-IFRS income tax benefit (expense), Non-IFRS diluted earnings per share (“EPS”), Non-IFRS adjusted EBITDA, Non-IFRS adjusted free cash flow, Non-IFRS total capital expenditures net of proceeds from government grants, and any related margins. We define each of Non-IFRS gross profit, Non-IFRS selling, general and administrative, Non-IFRS research and development, Non-IFRS operating profit, Non-IFRS other income (expense), Non-IFRS income tax benefit (expense) and Non-IFRS net income as gross profit, selling, general and administrative, research and development, operating profit, other income (expense), income tax benefit (expense), and net income (loss), respectively, adjusted for share-based compensation, structural optimization, amortization of acquired intangibles and other acquisition related charges, impairment charges, revaluation of equity investments, restructuring charges, issuance of shares to the USG, litigation claims, tax matters, and any associated income tax effects. We define Non-IFRS operating expense as Non-IFRS gross profit minus Non-IFRS operating profit. We define Non-IFRS diluted EPS as Non-IFRS net income divided by the diluted shares outstanding. We define Non-IFRS adjusted free cash flow as cash flow provided by (used in) operating activities less purchases of property, plant and equipment and intangible assets plus proceeds from government grants related to capital expenditures. We define Non-IFRS total capital expenditures net of proceeds from government grant as purchases of property, plant and equipment and intangible assets less proceeds of government grants. We define Non-IFRS adjusted EBITDA as net income (loss) adjusted for the impact of finance expense, finance income, income tax expense (benefit), depreciation and amortization, share-based compensation, restructuring charges, impairment charges, revaluation of equity investments, structural optimization, issuance of shares to the USG, litigation claims and acquisition related charges. We define each of Non-IFRS gross margin, Non-IFRS operating margin, Non-IFRS net income margin, Non-IFRS adjusted free cash flow margin and Non-IFRS adjusted EBITDA margin as Non-IFRS gross profit, Non-IFRS operating profit, Non-IFRS net income, Non-IFRS adjusted free cash flow and Non-IFRS adjusted EBITDA, respectively, divided by net revenue. Any adjustments described above that are zero for a given period are excluded from the “Reconciliation of IFRS to Non-IFRS” table. See "Reconciliation of IFRS to Non-IFRS" section for a detailed reconciliation of Non-IFRS financial measures to the most directly comparable IFRS measure.
We believe that in addition to our results determined in accordance with IFRS, these Non-IFRS financial measures provide useful information to both management and investors in measuring our financial performance and highlight trends in our business that may not otherwise be apparent when relying solely on IFRS measures. These Non-IFRS financial measures provide supplemental information regarding our operating performance that excludes certain gains, losses and non-cash charges that occur relatively infrequently and/or that we consider to be unrelated to our core operations. Management believes that Non-IFRS adjusted free cash flow as a Non-IFRS measure is helpful to investors as it provides insights into the nature and amount of cash the Company generates in the period.
Non-IFRS financial information is presented for supplemental informational purposes only and should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for financial information presented in accordance with IFRS. Our presentation of Non-IFRS measures should not be construed as an inference that our future results will be unaffected by unusual or nonrecurring items. Other companies in our industry may calculate these measures differently, which may limit their usefulness as comparative measures.
