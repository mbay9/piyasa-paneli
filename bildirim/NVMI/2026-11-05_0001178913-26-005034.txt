2026
Third Quarter Results
Total
revenues for the third quarter of 2026 were $287.8 million, an increase of 13% compared with the second quarter of 2026 and an increase
of 28% compared with the third quarter of 2025.
Gross
margin in the third quarter of 2026 was 57.7%, compared with 56.5% in the second quarter of 2026 and 56.7% in the third quarter of 2025.
Operating
expenses in the third quarter of 2026 were $73.3 million, compared with $68.0 million in the second quarter of 2026 and $63.6 million
in the third quarter of 2025.
On
a GAAP basis, the Company reported net income of $90.0 million, or $2.64 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2026. This is compared
with net income of $75.0 million, or $2.20 per diluted share, in the second quarter of 2026, and $61.4 million, or $1.90 per diluted
share, in the third quarter of 2025.
On
a non-GAAP basis, the Company reported net income of $98.4 million, or $2.86 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2026. This is
compared with net income of $86.5 million, or $2.51 per diluted share, in the second quarter of 2026, and $70.0 million, or $2.16 per
diluted share, in the third quarter of 2025.

About
Nova
Nova
is a leading innovator and key provider of material, optical and chemical metrology solutions for advanced process control in semiconductor
manufacturing. Nova delivers continuous innovation by providing state-of-the-art, high-performance metrology solutions for effective
process control throughout the semiconductor fabrication lifecycle. Nova’s product portfolio, which combines high-precision hardware
and cutting-edge software, provides its customers with deep insight into developing and producing the most advanced semiconductor devices.
Nova’s unique capability to deliver innovative solutions enables its customers to improve performance, enhance product yields and
accelerate time to market. Nova acts as a partner to semiconductor manufacturers from its offices worldwide. Additional information may
be found on Nova’s website link - https://www.novami.com/ .
Nova
is traded on the Nasdaq and TASE, Nasdaq ticker symbol NVMI.
