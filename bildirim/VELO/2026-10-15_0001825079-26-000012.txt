Velo3D Announces Preliminary Third Quarter 2026 Revenue of Approximately $19.5 Million to $20.5 Million, Representing Year-over-Year Growth of Approximately 43% to 51%
Company plans to report final financial results for Q3 2026 the week of November 9, 2026
FREMONT, Calif., Oct. 15, 2026 /PRNewswire/-- Velo3D, Inc. (Nasdaq: VELO) , a leading metal additive manufacturing technology company, today announced its preliminary unaudited revenue results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
The Company expects revenue for the third quarter of 2026 to be in the range of approximately $19.5 million to $20.5 million, representing year-over-year growth of approximately 43% to 51% compared with revenue of $13.6 million for the third quarter of 2025. These preliminary estimates are based on information available to management as of the date of this press release and have been prepared in good faith on a basis consistent with prior periods. These preliminary results are unaudited, are subject to the completion of the Company’s customary quarter-end financial closing procedures, final adjustments and other developments that may arise before the Company’s financial statements for the quarter are finalized. The Company's independent registered public accounting firm has not audited, reviewed or performed any procedures with respect to this preliminary revenue information and expresses no opinion or any other form of assurance with respect to it.
The Company’s actual results could differ materially from these preliminary estimates. This preliminary information is not necessarily indicative of the results to be expected for any future period and should not be viewed as a substitute for full financial statements prepared in accordance with Generally Accepted Accounting Principles in the United States (U.S. GAAP).
Velo3D expects to report its final financial results for the third quarter of 2026 during the week of November 9, 2026.
About Velo3D:
Velo3D is a metal 3D printing technology company that enables customers to build mission-critical metal parts. The fully integrated solution includes the Flow™ print preparation software, the Sapphire® family of printers and the Assure™ quality control system — all powered by Velo3D's Intelligent Fusion® manufacturing process. Learn more at velo3d.com.
Velo, Velo3D, Sapphire and Intelligent Fusion are registered trademarks of Velo3D, Inc. Flow and Assure are trademarks of Velo3D, Inc.
