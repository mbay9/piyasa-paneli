Sterling Reports Record Third Quarter Results and Raises Full Year 2026 Guidance
THE WOODLANDS, TX – October 28, 2026 – Sterling Infrastructure, Inc. (NasdaqGS: STRL) (“Sterling” or the “Company”) today announced strong financial results for the third quarter of 2026.
The financial comparisons herein are to the prior year quarter, unless otherwise noted.
Third Quarter 2026 Consolidated Results:
• Revenues of $1.56 billion, an increase of 127%.
• Net income of $214.9 million, or $6.92 per diluted share, both increases of 133%.
• EBITDA (1) of $320.8 million, an increase of 124%.
• Each of these third quarter 2026 metrics represent a new record.
Adjusted Results:
• Adjusted net income (1) of $235.4 million, or $7.58 per diluted share, increases of 119% and 118%, respectively.
• Adjusted EBITDA (1) of $337.8 million, an increase of 117%.
Cash Flow Metrics:
• Cash flows from operations totaled $427.1 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026.
• Cash and cash equivalents totaled $216.9 million at September 30, 2026.
• Share repurchases totaled $107.3 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026.
Backlog Metrics:
• Signed Backlog at September 30, 2026 was $4.14 billion, an increase of 61% from the prior year period.
• Combined Backlog (2) at September 30, 2026 was $6.14 billion, an increase of 78% from the prior year period.
• Third quarter 2026 book-to-burn ratios were 0.9x for Backlog and 1.4x for Combined Backlog.
Third Quarter 2026 Segment Results:
E-Infrastructure Solutions
• E-Infrastructure Solutions revenues of $1.30 billion, an increase of 212%.
◦ Revenues from Site Development operations increased 133%.
◦ The CEC electrical business, which was acquired on September 1, 2025, contributed $425.6 million of revenues in the quarter, compared with $41.4 million during the partial ownership period of the third quarter of 2025. On a pro forma basis assuming a full quarter of ownership in the third quarter of 2025, CEC revenues increased 305% year-over-year.

• Transportation Solutions operating income of $30.2 million, an increase of 24.0%. Transportation Solutions operating margin of 18.3%.

Building Solutions
• Building Solutions revenues of $96.0 million, a decline of 5.4%.
• Building Solutions operating income of $5.7 million, a decline of 47.3%. Building Solutions operating margin of 5.9%.
• Building Solutions adjusted operating income (1) of $7.7 million, a decline of 38.7%. Building Solutions adjusted operating margin of 8.0%.
CEO Remarks and Outlook
“We delivered another exceptional quarter, with adjusted net income increasing 119%, adjusted diluted EPS reaching $7.58, and adjusted EBITDA more than doubling. Revenue increased 127%, including 70% organic growth,” stated Joe Cutillo, Sterling’s Chief Executive Officer. “These results reflect the strength of our strategy and the disciplined execution of our teams.”
“Demand remains strong, and our multi-year visibility continues to expand. Together, our signed backlog, unsigned awards, and next phases in design on our current projects represent nearly $8 billion of scoped work, up approximately 90% year-over-year. Importantly, these metrics do not capture the full potential for additional phases and expanded scopes as these large, multi-year projects advance.”
Mr. Cutillo continued, “ E-Infrastructure Solutions continued to deliver, with revenue increasing 212% and adjusted operating income growing 171%, driven by exceptional performance across both our site development and electrical operations. Site Development generated significant growth across all regions, while maintaining strong operating margin. Demand for CEC’s electrical services remains extremely strong, and the business is scaling to capitalize on this opportunity. CEC revenue quadrupled on a pro forma year-over-year basis (2) and operating margin improved both year-over-year and sequentially. We now believe that CEC has the potential to generate over $1 billion in revenue in 2026.
Within E-Infrastructure, our signed backlog, unsigned awards and next phases in design now approach $7 billion, an increase of more than 130% year-over-year. Mission-critical projects, including data center campuses, manufacturing and semiconductor facilities, represent approximately 95% of this opportunity.
In Transportation Solutions, revenue declined 3%, as we continued to reallocate resources from transportation projects toward higher-margin E-Infrastructure opportunities. Adjusted operating income increased 22%, while adjusted operating margin expanded 410 basis points year-over-year to reach 19.7%, reflecting disciplined transportation project selection.
In Building Solutions , revenue declined 5%, reflecting slower homebuilder activity, while adjusted operating income decreased 39%. We expect market conditions to remain challenging through 2026 as housing affordability continues to weigh on demand, but remain optimistic about the long-term growth opportunities in key geographies.”

About Sterling
Sterling operates through a variety of subsidiaries within three segments specializing in E-Infrastructure, Transportation and Building Solutions in the United States, primarily across the Southern, Northeastern, Mid-Atlantic and Rocky Mountain regions and the Pacific Islands. E-Infrastructure Solutions provides advanced, large-scale site development services and mission-critical electrical services for data centers, semiconductor fabrication, manufacturing, distribution centers, warehousing, power generation and more. Transportation Solutions includes infrastructure and rehabilitation projects for highways, roads, bridges, airports, ports, rail and storm drainage systems. Building Solutions includes residential and commercial concrete foundations for single-family and multi-family homes, parking structures, elevated slabs, other concrete work, plumbing services, and surveys for new single-family residential builds. From strategy to operations, we are committed to sustainability by operating responsibly to safeguard and improve society’s quality of life. Non-GAAP Measures
This press release contains “Non-GAAP” financial measures as defined under Regulation G of the amended U.S. Securities Exchange Act of 1934. The Company reports financial results in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”), but the Company believes that certain Non-GAAP financial measures provide useful supplemental information to investors regarding the underlying business trends and performance of the Company’s ongoing operations and are useful for period-over-period comparisons of those operations.
Non-GAAP measures may include adjusted net income, adjusted operating income, adjusted segment operating income, adjusted EPS, EBITDA, and adjusted EBITDA, in each case excluding the impacts of certain identified items. The excluded items represent items that the Company does not consider to be representative of its normal operations. The Company believes that these measures are useful for investors to review, because they provide a consistent measure of the underlying financial results of the Company’s ongoing business and, in the Company’s view, allow for a supplemental comparison against historical results and expectations for future performance. Furthermore, the Company uses each of these to measure the performance of the Company’s operations for budgeting and forecasting, as well as for determining employee incentive compensation. However, Non-GAAP measures should not be considered as substitutes for net income, operating income, segment operating income, EPS, or other data prepared and reported in accordance with GAAP and should be viewed in addition to the Company’s reported results prepared in accordance with GAAP.
