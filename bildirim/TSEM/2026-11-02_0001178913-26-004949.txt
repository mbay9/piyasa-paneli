MIGDAL HAEMEK, ISRAEL – November
2, 2026 – Tower Semiconductor (NASDAQ/TASE: TSEM) reports today its results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter of 2026 Results Overview
Record revenue for the third quarter of 2026
of $526 million, representing revenue growth of 33% year over year, as compared to $396 million in the third quarter of 2025.
Record gross profit for the third quarter
of 2026 of $179 million, 92% higher than $93 million in the third quarter of 2025.
Operating profit for the third quarter of
2026 was a record of $127 million (excluding non-recurring items), a multiple of 2.5X as compared to the $51 million operating profit
for the third quarter of 2025.
Net profit for the third quarter of 2026
was a record of $120 million (excluding non-recurring items), representing 23% net margin and reflecting $1.06 basic and $1.05 diluted
earnings per share. This net profit is a multiple of 2.2X of the $54 million net profit in the third quarter of 2025.
Cash from operating activities in the third
quarter of 2026 was $185 million and investments in property and equipment, net, were $191 million. In the third quarter of 2025,
cash flow generated from operating activities was $139 million and investments in property and equipment, net, were $103 million.
Business Outlook
The Company provides revenue guidance of
$600 million for the fourth quarter of 2026, with an upward or downward range of 5%. Mid-range revenue guidance represents an increase
of 36% year-over-year and 14% quarter-over-quarter.
Russell Ellwanger, Chief Executive Officer
of Tower Semiconductor, said: “We are entering the strongest growth phase in our history, according to fourth quarter guidance,
closing the year at $2 billion annual revenue and a $2.4 billion annualized revenue run rate. We target to begin 2027 avoiding first quarter’s
typical industry seasonality, with clear visibility to incremental sequential revenues with accompanying margins’ growth.”
Ellwanger further added: “With a background
of growth and strong increasing demand in each of our core businesses, infrastructure (SiPho and SiGe) demand specifically is driving
exceptional growth in our high-speed connectivity business. We target to achieve an annualized $1.1 billion run rate in the fourth quarter
of 2026 of SiPho revenue alone, namely 5X of the 2025 SiPho annual revenue. Our on-going capacity investments, including the announced
Track-1 Japan 300mm buildout, are progressing ahead of schedule, supported by customer contracts which are being extended through 2029,
providing great assurance to achieve our 2028 revenue and margins model, and a well-defined path for accretive margins thereafter. The
combination of Tower’s technology leadership, expanding capacity and deep alignment with our customers, positions Tower to deliver
significant long-term growth and shareholder value.”

About Tower Semiconductor
Tower Semiconductor Ltd. (NASDAQ/TASE: TSEM)
is a leading foundry of high-value analog semiconductor solutions, serving growing markets including industrial, automotive, mobile,
infrastructure, medical, and aerospace & defense. The company offers a broad portfolio of advanced and innovative analog technologies,
including silicon photonics (SiPho), SiGe, RF CMOS, CMOS image sensors, power management, and mixed-signal, supported by world-class
design enablement and process transfer services. Tower operates a global manufacturing network with 200mm and 300mm fabs in Israel, the
U.S., Japan, and Italy, providing customers with scalable capacity and multi-fab sourcing. Learn more at www.towersemi.com .
