Forgent Reports Fiscal First Quarter 2027 Results Above High End of Guidance;
Raises Full-Year Outlook on Strong Commercial and Operational Execution
Fiscal First Quarter 2027 Highlights
• Revenues of $479 million , an increase of 69% year-over-year
• Net Income of $55 million, an increase of 252% year-over-year
• Adjusted EBITDA of $105 million, an increase of 61% year-over-year
• Net Income margin of 11.5%; Adjusted EBITDA margin of 21.9%
• Diluted EPS of $0.18; Adjusted EPS of $0.24
• Cash flow from operations of $136 million , an increase of $130 million year-over-year
Updated Full Year Fiscal 2027 Guidance
• Revenues in the range of $2,450 to $2,650 million, representing 80% YoY growth at the midpoint
• Adjusted EBITDA in the range of $587 to $637 million, representing 90% YoY growth at the midpoint
• Adjusted EPS in the range of $1.30 to $1.44, representing 101% YoY growth at the midpoint
DAYTON, MN - November 12, 2026 - Forgent Power Solutions, Inc. ("Forgent" or the "Company") (NYSE: FPS), a leading designer and manufacturer of electrical distribution equipment used in data centers, the power grid and energy-intensive industrial facilities, today announced financial results for its fiscal first quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Forgent reported first quarter revenues of $479 million, an increase of $195 million, or 69%, compared to the prior year’s quarter, the highest quarterly revenue in the Company’s history. As the Company communicated last month, first quarter results exceeded the high end of its guidance ranges for revenues and Adjusted EBITDA, as strong production ramp execution drove higher throughput and better cost absorption.
“Based on our strong first quarter commercial and operational execution, together with backlog that fully covers our fiscal 2027 guidance, we are raising our full-year outlook,” said Gary Niederpruem, Chief Executive Officer of Forgent. “Robust customer demand and strong execution on our production ramp give us greater confidence in our higher outlook. We are also gaining meaningful traction with multiple frontier AI labs and hyperscalers through our direct sales strategy, and we expect orders from these important customers to build through fiscal 2027 and into fiscal 2028.”
Mr. Niederpruem continued, “With much of our new manufacturing capacity now online, we are increasing our focus on performance optimization. The next phase for Forgent is about converting our expanded scale into greater operating leverage by standardizing processes, improving productivity, strengthening sourcing discipline, and driving more consistent execution across the business. With Amy Ard as Chief Financial Officer, Juan Macias as President of Solutions & Services, and Jamie Brown as President of Transformers, we have experienced financial and operational leadership in place to drive this work. As we execute these initiatives, we believe we are positioning Forgent for meaningful margin expansion over the next several years.”
Net Income for the first quarter was $55 million, an increase of $39 million or 252%, compared to the prior year’s quarter, and Net Income margin was 11.5%. Net Income increased primarily due to higher gross profit, partially offset by higher selling, general and administrative costs. Diluted EPS for the first quarter was $0.18 and Adjusted EPS was $0.24.
Adjusted EBITDA for the first quarter was $105 million, an increase of $40 million or 61%, compared to the prior year’s quarter. Adjusted EBITDA increased primarily due to higher gross profit, partially offset by higher selling, general and administrative costs. Adjusted EBITDA margin was 21.9% in the quarter, which was ahead of the Company’s expectations due to higher throughput and better cost absorption.
Cash flow from operations improved $130 million year-over-year to $136 million in the first quarter and capital expenditures were $32 million . First quarter cash flow from operations benefited from higher earnings and favorable timing of customer deposits and milestone payments, while the Company continued planned investment in working capital, primarily inventory, to support the production ramp.
Newly promoted Chief Financial Officer Amy Ard, who has helped build the Company’s finance platform since its formation, will play a key role in the Company’s focus on converting growth into stronger margins and cash flow. “Having been part of Forgent’s leadership from the beginning, I am incredibly honored to step into this role at such an important point in the Company’s evolution,” said Ms. Ard. “In my prior role as Executive Vice President of Finance, I helped build the systems, processes and financial infrastructure that support the platform we have today. This role gives me the opportunity to apply that foundation more directly to advance our performance optimization initiatives across margins, working capital and cash flow.”

About Forgent Power Solutions
Forgent (NYSE: FPS) is a leading U.S. designer and manufacturer of electrical distribution equipment used in data centers, the power grid and energy-intensive industrial facilities. The Company specializes in manufacturing custom products that are “engineered-to-order” for technically demanding applications. We believe Forgent is one of a small number of companies that can manufacture all of the electrical distribution equipment required for a data center or large manufacturing facility's powertrain with some of the highest levels of customization and shortest lead times available in the industry. For more information about Forgent, please visit us at forgentpower.com .
